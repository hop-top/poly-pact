# Admission cases

The `admission/` group holds the positive control `admission/valid` and the admission negatives of draft Section 22.2, with the identity admission cases of row CL-6.4. Every case uses the evaluation case format of [../README.md](../README.md), and its expected result is `expected/admission/<case>/decision.json`. The rows each case supports are in [../../spec-map.md](../../spec-map.md).

Each negative case is `admission/valid` with one change. The case keeps every other file as it is in `admission/valid`, except that it carries its own contract `jti` and `nonce` and its own presentation `jti`. Artifacts a case does not change are byte for byte those of `admission/valid`. `pactfix generate` signs every case. The cases of rows R08 to R10, R12 and R24 change no artifact: their change is an `exchange.json` (format v2, section Format v2 of [../README.md](../README.md)) that says how the harness submits the presentation, what does not answer, and when the evaluation ends.

## Reading the expected results

- `checks` lists only the check whose result the draft fixes for the change. Other checks may fail as a consequence, or go unevaluated; the case does not constrain their results. Every check the contract's `context_checks` names still appears in the receipt exactly once, `UNKNOWN` with `NOT_EVALUATED` when it was not evaluated (Section 12.1).
- A required condition that is demonstrably false gives `FAIL` and `INADMISSIBLE`. A requirement that cannot be established, such as an unsupported keyword or extension, gives `UNKNOWN` and `DEFERRED`, as in B.12 and Section 17.3.1.
- A failing check carries as its reason code the Section 13.4 code for its condition: `INVALID_CLAIM` for a value or claim that fails its rule, `INVALID_PROOF` for a failed holder binding, `CONTEXT_MISMATCH` for a principal, audience, realm or operation that differs, and `UNSUPPORTED_REQUIREMENT` for an unsupported field rule or extension.
- A presentation whose key is not the enrolled holder key has no valid holder signature (Section 7.2). Section 12.2 rejects it before an authenticated evaluation, with a transport error and no receipt, so its manifest outcome is `REJECTED`. The cases of row CL-6.4 apply the conditions of section 8 of [../../profiles/identity-mapping.md](../../profiles/identity-mapping.md) to the presented agent: its binding on the authenticated workload must be in force and name the presentation key (Section 6.2). A key that a rotation retired is not the enrolled key (IM-26), and a revoked binding is not in force (IM-18), so both fail the holder binding of Section 11.3, step 2, and get `INVALID_PROOF`. `authenticated.binding_status` carries the binding status of `resolve_agent_binding` (Section 17.1) in lower case, as `active` does in `admission/valid`. The other cases of row CL-6.4 take the form that section 8 gives their condition (section native_context.json of [../README.md](../README.md)): a transport error with no receipt, except `compromised-binding` and `enrollment-source-unavailable`, which are evaluated and get a receipt. The `security_holds` of every CL-6.4 case is empty.
- `receipt.claims` leaves out each claim whose value the change makes open, such as `tenant_id` in `wrong-tenant`. When the actual operation differs, `operation_digest` is the `object_digest` of the case's `actual_operation.json`, the operation the validator evaluated, and `operation_id` and `principal_id` are its values: `invoice-export-operation-0002` in `wrong-operation` and `https://identity.example/principals/bob` in `wrong-principal` (Sections 12.1 and 17.1). `requirements_digest` is the `artifact_digest` of the case's own `signed/contract.jws`.
- `exp_max` is `1792080060`, 60 seconds after the evaluation time, the bound Section 12.1 sets for every receipt, except in the two cases of row R24. There the receipt is issued when the evaluation ends, at the deadline 1792080030 or at the cancellation 1792080010, so `exp_max` is 1792080090 and 1792080070, and `receipt.claims` leaves out `iat`.
- A case with several submissions lists one expected response per submission under `responses`, and a case with handoff attempts lists their results under `handoff` (section Format v2 of [../README.md](../README.md)). `subject` names the response that the manifest `outcome` summarizes: the rejected submission or handoff, so the outcome of rows R08 to R10 is `REJECTED`.
- A replayed presentation is not accepted for evaluation (Section 22.2), and a presentation whose time bounds have passed when it arrives is rejected with `EXPIRED` (Section 12.2). Both get a transport error and no receipt.
- In `attestation-reuse` the handoff for another request comes first, while the attestation is unconsumed, so its only failure is the operation digest. Section 11.5 marks the `jti` consumed before the gateway accepts the attestation for its own operation, so that rejected attempt consumes nothing, the next handoff is accepted, and the one after it is a replay.
- In the R24 cases the evaluation ends before `native_reads_at`, so every native read is outstanding: the reads of `read_native_claim` for the field rules `principal`, `roles` and `supervisor-approval`, and the `check_context` calls of `purpose-operation-v1`, `native-delegation-v1` and `security-hold-v1` (Sections 12.1, 17.1). None of those checks was performed (Section 12.1). The cases list the three context checks `UNKNOWN` with `NOT_EVALUATED`, which Section 12.1 fixes for an unevaluated `context_checks` entry; the three field rules are not listed, since Section 12.1 fixes only that they are not recorded as passed, and the runner judges that (section Runner contract of [../README.md](../README.md)). `native-principal-binding-v1` needs no native read (Section 17.5), and native authentication completes when the presentation arrives, so the evaluation is authenticated.
