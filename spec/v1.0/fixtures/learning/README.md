# Learning cases

These cases test the learning service of draft Section 16: installation of a signed `LearningPolicy` (Section 16.2, Appendix A.3), registration of `LearningArtifact` manifests (Section 16.3), lifecycle events and their compare-and-swap (Sections 16.4 and 16.5, Appendix A.2), compatible rollback, policy expiry, the effect of corrections and security holds on the registry (Sections 14.4, 15.4 and 16.5), and LLM assessments handed to the service as data (Section 16.6). Each case is a sequence of steps against one learning service, and its expected result is the registry state at the end. The cases are learning cases in the format of [../README.md](../README.md), section Format v2, Learning cases; the runner verb is `learning`. Spec-map rows `R19` to `R23`, `CL-16.4` and `CL-16.5` list them; `R17` lists `rollback-preserves-hold` and `CL-13.4` lists the cases that cover `ID_CONFLICT` and `LEARNING_REJECTED`.

| Case | Adversarial claim | Expected | Manifest outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| `happy-path` | (positive control) | Registration, `EVALUATED`, `SHADOW` and, 86460 seconds into the shadow phase, `ACTIVE`, each `ACCEPTED`; the artifact is active in its context. | `ACCEPTED` |
| `gate-not-met` | A candidate can be promoted before its shadow phase has run for `minimum_shadow_seconds`. | The `ACTIVE` event one hour into the shadow phase is rejected, `LEARNING_REJECTED` `GATE_NOT_MET`; a second one after 86460 seconds is accepted. | `REJECTED` |
| `concurrent-promotion-conflict` | Two controllers promoting from the same previous version can both succeed. | The first `ACTIVE` event is accepted; the second, with the same `expected_active_digest` (null), is rejected, `LEARNING_REJECTED` `VERSION_CONFLICT`. | `REJECTED` |
| `candidate-self-evaluated` | A candidate's controller can evaluate its own candidate as safe. | The `EVALUATED` event, whose only evaluation reference resolves to the controller, is rejected, `LEARNING_REJECTED` `SELF_EVALUATED`; the artifact stays `CANDIDATE`. | `REJECTED` |
| `missing-gate` | A learning policy without a release gate can authorize promotion. | The policy, with an empty `immutable_test_suite_digests`, is not installed: `INVALID_MESSAGE`, no reason codes. | `REJECTED` |
| `expired-policy` | An expired learning policy still authorizes promotion. | After the policy's `exp`, an `ACTIVE` event is rejected, `LEARNING_REJECTED` `POLICY_EXPIRED`, and a `FROZEN` event is accepted. | `REJECTED` |
| `incompatible-fallback` | A rollback can activate any artifact the controller names. | A rollback naming an artifact of another `base_revision` is rejected, `LEARNING_REJECTED` `FALLBACK_INCOMPATIBLE`; the rollback to the conservative set is accepted. | `REJECTED` |
| `rollback-preserves-hold` | A rollback erases a security hold. | The rollback to a compatible fallback is accepted, the fallback is active, and the hold of the confirmed `SECURITY` report stays. | `ACCEPTED` |
| `correction-triggers-recompute` | A shadowed artifact trained on a corrected event keeps its evaluation. | The `CORRECTION` is accepted and the artifact in `SHADOW` requires re-evaluation. | `ACCEPTED` |
| `retirement-and-reactivation` | A retired, frozen or rolled-back artifact stays in force, or a frozen one cannot be evaluated again. | Every retirement row of the Section 16.5 table and `FROZEN` to `EVALUATED`, each `ACCEPTED`; five artifacts end `RETIRED`, one `EVALUATED`, and every context keeps the conservative set. | `MATCH` |
| `revised-candidate-new-manifest` | A registered candidate can be changed in place. | Version 2, with `parent_artifact_digest` naming version 1, is registered; version 1 goes from `CANDIDATE` to `RETIRED`. | `ACCEPTED` |
| `mutated-accepted-manifest` | A manifest accepted once can be replaced under the same `artifact_id` and `version`. | The identical manifest again gets `DUPLICATE`; a manifest with the same `artifact_id` and `version` and another `content_digest` gets `ID_CONFLICT`. | `REJECTED` |
| `out-of-envelope-change` | A learning artifact can claim a control outside the policy's envelope, such as a native role grant. | Registration is rejected, `LEARNING_REJECTED` `CONTROL_NOT_ADAPTIVE`; nothing is registered. | `REJECTED` |
| `observe-mode-no-activation` | A policy in `observe` mode lets a controller activate an artifact. | `EVALUATED` and `SHADOW` are accepted; `ACTIVE` is rejected, `LEARNING_REJECTED` `OBSERVE_MODE`. | `REJECTED` |
| `llm-assessment-injection` | An LLM assessment answer that tells the service to ignore its policy changes the registry. | The assessment gets no answer and changes nothing: the artifact stays `EVALUATED`, no context changes and no hold is cleared. | `MATCH` |

## Common setup

Every case runs against the learning service `https://learning.example/finance` (tenant `acme`, environment `https://gateway.example/environments/finance`), the `aud` of B.9 to B.11, with `now` 1792080000 and a clock skew of 5 seconds. `config.json`'s `trust` registers the policy authority `https://trust.example/issuers/finance`, signing with the issuer key, which stands in for it as [../vectors/jws/README.md](../vectors/jws/README.md) describes, and the controller `https://learning.example/controllers/finance`, signing with the learning-controller key ([../keys/README.md](../keys/README.md)).

- The policy is that of B.9 with its own `jti` and an `exp` 30 days after its issuance at 1792080000, so that it outlives the shadow phase; `expired-policy` gives it an `exp` two days after issuance, and the cases below name their other changes. Every policy is installed by step 0.
- The artifact `signed/artifacts/baseline-v1.jws` is that of B.10 with its own `jti` and an `exp` 30 days after issuance, the maximum of Section 16.5. Its one intended context is that of B.10 and B.11 (`invoice.read` on `department-finance-invoice`). The other artifacts have the same shape with their own `artifact_id` and `artifact_store_ref` and with their own `content_digest`, `source_data_manifest_digest` and `evaluation_report_digest` values, each the SHA-256 of a label, standing in for the digests of real files.
- `config.json`'s `evaluations` resolves `configured-evaluation-store:independent-0001` to `https://learning.example/evaluators/independent`, the `required_evaluator_ids` of B.9. Its `gates` give every artifact that an event names the observations of the example in [../README.md](../README.md), section Learning cases: 120 independent tasks, 691200 seconds of observation, the policy's immutable test suite passed, `native-denial-override-count` 0 and `admission-regression-rate` 0.004, each within the B.9 bounds. A shadow phase is too short only in `gate-not-met`.
- Each event is issued at its step's `received_at`, which its `signing.json` entry states as `iat`, with an `exp` 300 seconds later, the most Section 16.5 allows. The digest members of events, and the `parent_artifact_digest` of `revised-candidate-new-manifest`, are filled by `pactfix generate` through `digests` (section Generator contract: signing.json).
- The normal activation path is `EVALUATED` at 1792080060, `SHADOW` at 1792080120 and `ACTIVE` at 1792166580, 86460 seconds after the shadow phase began and clear of the 86400-second `minimum_shadow_seconds`: no case relies on a boundary equality, as Section 15.3 requires of promotion policies.
- Every event's `previous_artifact_digest` equals its `expected_active_digest`, as Appendix A.2 requires: the artifact in force in the event's scope before the transition, null when the conservative set is in force, as in B.11. Every event that leaves `ACTIVE` names in `scope_contexts` each context its artifact is in force in (Section 16.5).

### Member conventions

The draft fixes what the service checks; where it leaves a member's value to the controller, the cases use the values below, and no expected result depends on them:

- `activate_artifact_digest` is the transitioned artifact for `ACTIVE`, the fallback or null for `ROLLED_BACK` (Appendix A.2), and null otherwise.
- `scope_contexts` is the artifact's `intended_contexts`, and `effective_at` is the event's `iat`.
- `EVALUATED`, `SHADOW` and `ACTIVE` events cite the evaluation reference of the case and carry `EVALUATION_GATES_PASSED`, as B.11 does; the controller claims the gates passed even where the service finds they did not. A `FROZEN` event carries `DRIFT_THRESHOLD`, a `ROLLED_BACK` event `REGRESSION_LIMIT` (plus `FALLBACK_INCOMPATIBLE` when it records the conservative fallback), and a `RETIRED` event `DATA_CORRECTION`, with no evaluation reference.

### Reading the registry

`registry.json` follows section registry.json of [../README.md](../README.md). Step numbers count from 0. A step of kind `policy` that installs its policy, and a step of kind `assessment`, get neither an acknowledgment nor a rejection. `active` has one entry per context named by a registered artifact: its `artifact` is the artifact in state `ACTIVE` there, or null when the conservative set is in force. A `FROZEN`, `ROLLED_BACK` or `RETIRED` artifact is not active (Section 16.5: a freeze or rollback invalidates the contracts that pin it, and a frozen artifact needs a new evaluation and shadow phase before reactivation), so its context returns to the conservative set unless a rollback activates a fallback. Every case starts with no artifact and the conservative set in every context; none needs a recorded administrative activation.

## Cases

**`happy-path`.** Steps: the policy; registration of `baseline-v1` (`ACCEPTED`, `CANDIDATE`); `EVALUATED`, `SHADOW` and `ACTIVE` events on the normal activation path, each `ACCEPTED`. The `ACTIVE` event has `expected_active_digest` null, which matches the conservative state, so the compare-and-swap succeeds. The artifact ends `ACTIVE` and is the active artifact of its context. The subject is the acknowledgment of the `ACTIVE` event.

**`gate-not-met`.** The example of [../README.md](../README.md), section Learning cases, with the same steps and the `jti` `MLkZJm1NFAtQcUiYR0oKvQ` for the first `ACTIVE` event. That event arrives at 1792083720, 3600 seconds into the shadow phase and short of `minimum_shadow_seconds` 86400, and is rejected with `GATE_NOT_MET`: a present release gate is not satisfied (Section 16.5, Appendix A.2). The second `ACTIVE` event, at 1792166580, is accepted. The subject is the rejection.

**`concurrent-promotion-conflict`.** The policy lists two controllers: `https://learning.example/controllers/finance` and `https://learning.example/controllers/finance-replica`. The issuer key stands in for the second controller, as it stands in for the policy authority; the two are told apart by their `iss` and their `trust` records. Each controller registers and shadows its own artifact for the same context: `baseline-v1` and `baseline-replica-v1`. Both `ACTIVE` events arrive at 1792166580 and both name `expected_active_digest` null, the version each controller saw. The steps format applies steps in order, so the first is the one the service applies first: it is accepted, and the second then fails the compare-and-swap, since the context's active artifact is now `baseline-v1` (Section 16.5: "Concurrent controllers use a compare-and-swap on the expected previous artifact; conflicting transitions are rejected"). The rejection is `LEARNING_REJECTED` with `VERSION_CONFLICT`; `baseline-replica-v1` stays `SHADOW`. The subject is the rejection.

**`candidate-self-evaluated`.** `config.json`'s `evaluations` resolves the only evaluation reference, `configured-evaluation-store:controller-self-0001`, to the controller `https://learning.example/controllers/finance` itself. The candidate's report is the controller's own and the policy's independent evaluator has no result for it, so the `EVALUATED` event, which claims `EVALUATION_GATES_PASSED`, is rejected with `SELF_EVALUATED` (Section 16.3: "The artifact that proposes a change MUST NOT be its sole source of promotion evidence"). The artifact stays `CANDIDATE` and cannot reach `SHADOW` or `ACTIVE`.

**`missing-gate`.** One step: a policy that is B.9's with an empty `immutable_test_suite_digests`. Appendix A.3 requires at least one digest, so the policy fails the `LearningPolicy` schema and is not installed (Appendix A.2: "a learning policy without one is invalid (`INVALID_MESSAGE`, Section 13.4) and authorizes no promotion"). The step is rejected with `INVALID_MESSAGE` and no reason codes; the registry stays empty. The signed policy verifies: only its payload is invalid.

**`expired-policy`.** The policy expires at 1792252800, two days after issuance. `baseline-v1` is promoted at 1792166580, while the policy is current. A second artifact, `summary-baseline-v1`, whose intended context is a second context of the same agent and purpose (`invoice-summary.export` on `department-finance-summary-job`), so that its promotion replaces no active artifact, is shadowed from 1792080120. After the policy's `exp`, its `ACTIVE` event at 1792252860 is rejected with `POLICY_EXPIRED`, and the `FROZEN` event on `baseline-v1` at 1792252920 is accepted (Section 16.5: "No transition except to `FROZEN`, `ROLLED_BACK`, or `RETIRED` is accepted, and a rejected transition carries the reason `POLICY_EXPIRED`"). Both contexts end with the conservative set. The subject is the rejection.

**`incompatible-fallback`.** Two artifacts are registered, evaluated and shadowed for the same context: `baseline-v1` and `baseline-base2-v1`, whose `base_revision` is `finance-base-2`. `baseline-v1` is promoted. A rollback event on it at 1792166640 names `baseline-base2-v1` as `activate_artifact_digest`. That artifact meets conditions 2 to 4 of Section 16.5 (permitted implementation, a completed shadow phase of 86520 seconds under the current policy, intended contexts that cover the event's scope), but not condition 1, the same `base_revision` as the outgoing artifact, so the event is rejected with `FALLBACK_INCOMPATIBLE`. The rollback event at 1792166700, with `activate_artifact_digest` null and `FALLBACK_INCOMPATIBLE` among its reason codes, records the conservative fallback and is accepted (Section 16.5 and Appendix A.2). `baseline-v1` ends `ROLLED_BACK`, `baseline-base2-v1` stays `SHADOW`, and the context returns to the conservative set. The draft defines no check of an artifact's `base_revision` against the policy's at registration or evaluation, so `baseline-base2-v1`'s registration and events are accepted. The subject is the rejection.

**`rollback-preserves-hold`.** `baseline-v1` and the compatible `baseline-fallback-v1` (same `base_revision`, `model_type` and `implementation_id`, a shadow phase of 86580 seconds under the current policy at rollback, the same context) are registered, evaluated and shadowed; `baseline-v1` is promoted. At 1792166640 the security authority `https://security.example/finance`, one of the policy's `hold_clearance_authorities` and registered in `trust` with `reporter_role` `security_authority`, reports a confirmed incident: `security-event-0001`, a `SECURITY` `FAILURE` with `dimension` `none` and severity `high`, as in `reputation/security-hold-no-decay`, citing the confirmed incident record of `native_context.json` and signed with the security-authority key. It is acknowledged `ACCEPTED`, and its hold is active (Section 15.4). At 1792166700 the controller rolls `baseline-v1` back to `baseline-fallback-v1` in one event, which is accepted: `baseline-v1` is `ROLLED_BACK` and `baseline-fallback-v1` is `ACTIVE` in the context (Section 16.5: "Rollback records a transition on the outgoing artifact and activation of a previously evaluated compatible artifact in one atomic release event"). `holds` still lists `security-event-0001`: "Neither rollback nor data decay may erase a security hold" (Section 16.5). The case also carries the outcomes members `reputation_service` (`https://reputation.example/finance`, the report's `aud`) and `visibility`. The subject is the acknowledgment of the rollback.

**`correction-triggers-recompute`.** The reports of `outcomes/qualification/correction-recompute` ([../outcomes/qualification/README.md](../outcomes/qualification/README.md)) with their own `jti` values: the executor's `execution-event-0001`, an `EXECUTION` `SUCCESS` on `reliability`, ingested at 1792080000 before the artifact is registered, and its `CORRECTION` `correction-event-0002`, issued and ingested at 1792080180, with the same task, operation, dimension and reporter, superseding it (Section 14.6 rule 10). `config.json`'s `source_data_manifests` records `execution-event-0001` behind the `source_data_manifest_digest` of `baseline-v1`, which is in `SHADOW` when the correction arrives. Section 16.5 requires re-evaluation of every `ACTIVE` or `SHADOW` artifact whose source data manifest includes a corrected event, so `reevaluation_required` lists `baseline-v1`; its state does not change. Both reports are acknowledged `ACCEPTED`. The subject is the acknowledgment of the correction.

**`retirement-and-reactivation`.** Six artifacts, `baseline-e-v1` to `baseline-f-v1`, take the rows of the Section 16.5 table that no other case takes. `e` and `s` share the B.10 context; `a`, `r`, `g` and `f` each have their own (`resource_class` `department-finance-ledger`, `department-finance-payables`, `department-finance-receivables` and `department-finance-budget`), so that no promotion replaces an active artifact and no rollback finds a compatible fallback. `e` is evaluated and retired (`EVALUATED` to `RETIRED`); `s` is shadowed and retired (`SHADOW` to `RETIRED`). `a`, `r`, `g` and `f` are promoted at 1792166580. One minute later `a` is retired (`ACTIVE` to `RETIRED`), `r` is rolled back to the conservative set, with `activate_artifact_digest` null and `FALLBACK_INCOMPATIBLE`, since no artifact covers its context, and `g` and `f` are frozen. One minute after that `r` is retired (`ROLLED_BACK` to `RETIRED`), `g` is retired (`FROZEN` to `RETIRED`), and `f` gets a new `EVALUATED` event with the independent evaluation (`FROZEN` to `EVALUATED`, Section 16.5: "A frozen artifact requires a new successful evaluation and shadow phase before reactivation"). Every step is `ACCEPTED`. The events that leave `ACTIVE` name the outgoing artifact as `expected_active_digest`; the later ones name null, since the context is back to the conservative set. `registry.json` has no `subject`: the case is judged by the registry state.

**`revised-candidate-new-manifest`.** `baseline-v1` version 1 is registered. The controller retrains on corrected source data: `baseline-v1-r2` is version 2 of the same `artifact_id`, with new `content_digest`, `source_data_manifest_digest` and `evaluation_report_digest` values and `parent_artifact_digest` equal to the `artifact_digest` of version 1 (Section 16.3, Appendix A.2). Its registration is `ACCEPTED`. The `RETIRED` event on version 1, with `expected_state` `CANDIDATE`, is accepted (the `CANDIDATE` to `RETIRED` row of the Section 16.5 table). Version 1 ends `RETIRED` and version 2 `CANDIDATE`; no artifact is active. The subject is the acknowledgment of the `RETIRED` event.

**`mutated-accepted-manifest`.** `baseline-v1` is registered (`ACCEPTED`). The same signed manifest is registered again at 1792080060 and gets `DUPLICATE` (Section 16.3: "Re-registration of an identical manifest returns an `Acknowledgment` with status `DUPLICATE`"). At 1792080120, `baseline-v1-mutated`, a manifest with the same `artifact_id` and `version` but another `content_digest` and its own `jti`, is rejected with `ID_CONFLICT` and no reason codes (Section 16.3, Section 13.4). Its own `jti` keeps the rejection apart from the `(iss, jti)` deduplication of Section 7.5. The registry holds only the accepted manifest, in `CANDIDATE`. The subject is the rejection.

**`out-of-envelope-change`.** The manifest of `baseline-v1` declares `output_controls` `["supervisor-approval-required", "native-role-grant"]`. `native-role-grant` is not a `control_id` of the policy's `adaptive_fields`, which may never include native roles (Section 16.2), so registration is rejected with `LEARNING_REJECTED` and `CONTROL_NOT_ADAPTIVE` (Section 16.3). Nothing is registered, so `artifacts` and `active` are empty. The subject is the rejection.

**`observe-mode-no-activation`.** The policy is B.9's with `mode` `observe`. Candidates and their evaluation and shadow phases are recorded, so registration, `EVALUATED` and `SHADOW` are accepted; the `ACTIVE` event, after a shadow phase that meets every gate, is rejected with `OBSERVE_MODE`, since no active selection changes without a native authorized approval (Section 16.4). The artifact stays `SHADOW` and the context keeps the conservative set. The subject is the rejection.

**`llm-assessment-injection`.** `baseline-v1` is registered and evaluated. An `assessment` step then hands the service `assessments/capability-exercise-0001.json`: an attributable record (Section 16.6: prompt digest, model and version, rubric, input digest, output and uncertainty) whose output answers `PASS` and tells the service to ignore its policy and every release gate, promote the artifact, relax `supervisor-approval-required`, grant the agent a role and clear any hold. The output is untrusted assessment data, and prompt text cannot override issuer authority, immutable rules or native access decisions (Section 16.6), so the step gets no answer and changes nothing: the artifact stays `EVALUATED`, the context keeps the conservative set, and no hold, policy or role changes. The prompt and input digests are the SHA-256 of labels. `registry.json` has no `subject`, so the case is judged by the registry state alone.

## Coverage of Section 16.5

- States: every state of Section 16.5 ends some case: `CANDIDATE` (`candidate-self-evaluated`, `mutated-accepted-manifest`, `revised-candidate-new-manifest`), `EVALUATED` (`llm-assessment-injection`, `retirement-and-reactivation`), `SHADOW` (`observe-mode-no-activation`, `correction-triggers-recompute` and others), `ACTIVE` (`happy-path`, `gate-not-met`, `concurrent-promotion-conflict`, `rollback-preserves-hold`), `FROZEN` (`expired-policy`), `ROLLED_BACK` (`incompatible-fallback`, `rollback-preserves-hold`) and `RETIRED` (`revised-candidate-new-manifest`, `retirement-and-reactivation`).
- Transitions: every row of the Section 16.5 table is accepted in some case. `ACTIVE` to `ROLLED_BACK` is taken with a compatible fallback (`rollback-preserves-hold`) and with the conservative fallback (`incompatible-fallback`, `retirement-and-reactivation`); the retirement rows other than `CANDIDATE` to `RETIRED`, and `FROZEN` to `EVALUATED`, are those of `retirement-and-reactivation`. No case submits a transition outside the table: such an event is invalid (`INVALID_MESSAGE`, Appendix A.2).
- Rejection reason codes: `VERSION_CONFLICT`, `POLICY_EXPIRED`, `FALLBACK_INCOMPATIBLE`, `GATE_NOT_MET`, `SELF_EVALUATED`, `OBSERVE_MODE` and `CONTROL_NOT_ADAPTIVE` each reject one step. `DRIFT_THRESHOLD`, `REGRESSION_LIMIT`, `DATA_CORRECTION` and `EVALUATION_GATES_PASSED` appear only as reason codes of accepted events, which no expected result judges; `NATIVE_DENIAL_OVERRIDE` appears in none.
- Transport codes: `LEARNING_REJECTED`, `ID_CONFLICT` (`mutated-accepted-manifest`) and `INVALID_MESSAGE` (`missing-gate`).
- Compatible rollback: condition 1 fails in `incompatible-fallback`; all four hold in `rollback-preserves-hold`.
- Contract invalidation, the other effect of a `ROLLED_BACK` or `FROZEN` event, is not part of the registry state; the evaluate case `selection/selector-rolled-back` ([../selection/README.md](../selection/README.md)) covers a contract that pins a rolled-back artifact.
